隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

Libzora 是本地优先的电子书阅读与书库工具。你的书籍、封面、阅读进度、书签和批注默认只保存在你自己的设备上；应用不内置广告或统计分析组件，不会自动上传日志或崩溃报告。只有在你主动使用同步、AI、插件、检查更新或账号等联网功能时，才会产生下文所述的数据传输。

一、适用范围

本政策适用于 Libzora 的 Windows、macOS、Linux、Android、iOS / iPadOS 客户端，Libzora 官网（libzora.stacker.beauty）以及 Libzora 账号服务（以下合称“本服务”）。本服务由 Libzora 的开发者 kingstacker（以下称“我们”）提供。

你自行配置和使用的第三方服务（例如对象存储、WebDAV、iCloud、AI 服务商、插件访问的站点）由相应的服务提供方按其各自的隐私政策处理数据，不在本政策的覆盖范围内。

二、保存在你设备上的数据

以下数据由应用在你的设备本地保存，我们无法访问：

  • 你导入的书籍文件、封面和书籍信息；
  • 阅读进度、阅读时长统计、书签、划线、批注和读后感；
  • 词典、字体、朗读语音模型等你添加或下载的资源；
  • 应用设置，以及你填写的同步连接信息、同步密钥和 AI 服务的 API Key（使用系统提供的安全存储保护）；
  • 用于排查故障的本地软件日志。日志在写入前会做脱敏处理，只记录必要的错误事件和版本、平台信息，不记录书籍正文，也不会自动上传；只有你主动导出并分享时才会离开设备。

在 iOS / iPadOS 上，桌面小组件显示的阅读信息只在本机的应用与小组件之间共享。

三、何时会联网，以及传输什么

检查更新与资源下载。应用检查新版本，以及下载你选择的朗读语音模型、Calibre 安装信息等资源时，会访问 GitHub 和 Libzora 官网。与访问任何网站一样，对方服务器会收到你的 IP 地址和基本的请求信息（如应用版本和系统类型）。这些请求不包含你的书库或阅读数据。

跨设备同步。开启同步后，应用把书籍、封面、阅读进度、书签和批注直接传输到你自己选择的存储：自备的 S3 兼容存储、WebDAV，或你 Apple 账户下的 iCloud。数据不经过、也不保存在我们的服务器上。设置同步密钥后，数据会先在你的设备上加密再上传；同步密钥只保存在你的设备上，我们不持有也无法找回。即使启用加密，存储服务提供方仍可能看到文件数量、大致大小和同步时间。

AI 功能。使用 AI 阅读助手、翻译等功能时，应用会把完成该请求所需的内容（例如你选中的文字、相关书籍片段和你的提问）发送到你自己配置的 AI 服务商。我们不中转也不保存这些内容。

插件。插件只有在你安装并授权后才能访问网络。例如书籍信息匹配类插件会把书名、作者等检索词发送给它所查询的站点。

Kindle 相关功能。通过 USB 管理 Kindle 设备时，数据只在你的电脑与设备之间传输。使用“发送到 Kindle”时，你是在应用内直接登录并使用 Amazon 的服务，相关数据由 Amazon 按其政策处理。

问题反馈。应用内的反馈入口只是指向 GitHub Issues 和用户交流群的链接，应用不会自动附带任何信息。你在这些公开渠道发布的内容可能被他人查看，请在分享日志或截图前检查其中的个人信息。

四、账号服务处理的信息

注册并登录 Libzora 账号是可选的。如果你使用账号，账号服务会处理以下信息：

  • 账号资料：用户名、邮箱地址和经过安全处理的登录密码；
  • 设备与登录会话：应用为每台设备随机生成的设备标识（不读取硬件序列号）、设备名称与类型、登录时间和最近访问时间，用于展示和管理已登录设备；
  • 权益状态：账号的试用与权益开通情况及其有效期；
  • 安全信息：注册和登录时的 IP 地址，用于限制批量注册、防止滥用和保障账号安全。

这些信息仅用于身份验证、邮箱验证与密码找回、设备管理、权益校验和账号安全。账号服务不接收你的书籍、封面、阅读数据、批注、同步数据、同步密钥、存储连接凭据或 AI 服务的 API Key。

账号分为中国区与国际区，两个区域的账号数据分别存储，互不复制、合并或互通。

五、官网

Libzora 官网是静态页面，不使用广告或统计分析脚本，也不设置用于跟踪的 Cookie。页面只在你的浏览器本地保存语言偏好，并向 GitHub 查询最新版本信息；安装包直接从 GitHub 下载。

六、我们如何共享信息

我们不出售你的个人信息，也不会把它用于广告或用户画像。除以下情形外，我们不会向第三方提供账号信息：

  • 为提供服务所必需，例如通过邮件服务向你的邮箱发送验证码；
  • 依据法律法规或有权机关的强制性要求；
  • 事先获得你的明确同意。

七、保存期限

  • 本地数据由你自行保管，卸载应用或删除数据目录即可清除；
  • 存放在你自备存储或 iCloud 中的同步数据由你自行管理和删除，停止使用 Libzora 不会自动清除这些数据；
  • 账号信息在账号存续期间保存。停用账号后，我们会撤销全部登录会话；为保障安全、防止滥用并履行法定义务，仍会保留必要的账号与权益记录；
  • 用于限制批量注册的来源地址记录最长保留 30 天。

八、你的权利

你可以在应用的账户中心查看和修改用户名、修改密码、查看并移除已登录设备，或停用账号。你也可以通过下方的联系方式，请求查阅、更正或删除我们持有的你的账号信息，或撤回此前的同意；我们会依照适用法律处理。对于只保存在你设备或你自备存储中的数据，你可以随时直接导出或删除。

九、信息安全

账号服务与客户端之间的通信使用 HTTPS；登录密码不以明文保存；API Key 等敏感配置使用操作系统提供的安全存储保护；完整数据备份和同步数据支持端到端加密。请妥善保管你的同步密钥：遗失后，已加密的同步数据和备份将无法解密，重置账号密码也无法找回它。

十、未成年人

如果你是未满 14 周岁的未成年人，请在监护人的同意和指导下使用本服务，并且不要自行注册账号。如果我们发现在未获监护人同意的情况下收集了相关信息，会尽快删除。

十一、政策更新

我们可能随功能变化更新本政策。更新后的版本会发布在本页面并更新生效日期；涉及重大变更时，我们会在应用或官网以显著方式提示。

十二、联系我们

如对本政策或你的个人信息有任何疑问或请求，可以在 GitHub Issues 留言，或加入 QQ 用户群 1109898894 联系我们。请不要在公开渠道发布密码、密钥等敏感信息。

另请参阅 用户协议。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Libzora is a local-first ebook reader and library manager. Your books, covers, reading progress, bookmarks and annotations stay on your own devices by default. The app contains no advertising or analytics components and never uploads logs or crash reports automatically. Data leaves your device only when you choose to use a networked feature such as sync, AI, plugins, update checks or an account, as described below.

1. Scope

This policy covers the Libzora apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS / iPadOS, the Libzora website (libzora.stacker.beauty) and the Libzora account service (together, the “Service”). The Service is provided by kingstacker, the developer of Libzora (“we”, “us”).

Third-party services that you configure and use yourself — such as object storage, WebDAV, iCloud, AI providers and the sites that plugins access — handle data under their own privacy policies and are outside the scope of this policy.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is kept locally by the app. We have no access to it:

  • The book files you import, their covers and metadata;
  • Reading progress, reading-time statistics, bookmarks, highlights, notes and reflections;
  • Dictionaries, fonts, text-to-speech voice models and other resources you add or download;
  • App settings, plus the sync connection details, sync key and AI API keys you enter (protected by the secure storage your operating system provides);
  • A local software log used for troubleshooting. Entries are redacted before they are written and record only essential error events and version and platform information — never book content. The log is never uploaded automatically and leaves your device only if you export and share it yourself.

On iOS / iPadOS, the reading information shown in Home Screen widgets is shared only between the app and its widgets on the same device.

3. When the app goes online and what it sends

Update checks and resource downloads. When the app checks for a new version, or downloads resources you choose such as voice models or Calibre setup information, it contacts GitHub and the Libzora website. As with any web request, those servers receive your IP address and basic request information such as the app version and operating system. These requests contain nothing from your library or reading data.

Sync across devices. When you turn on sync, the app transfers books, covers, reading progress, bookmarks and annotations directly to storage that you choose: your own S3-compatible storage, WebDAV, or iCloud under your Apple Account. The data does not pass through and is not stored on our servers. If you set a sync key, data is encrypted on your device before upload; the key stays on your devices, and we neither hold it nor can recover it. Even with encryption, your storage provider may still see the number of files, their approximate sizes and when syncs happen.

AI features. When you use the AI reading assistant, translation or similar features, the app sends the content needed for that request — such as the text you selected, relevant passages from the book and your question — to the AI provider you configured. We do not relay or store this content.

Plugins. Plugins can access the network only after you install them and grant permission. A metadata-matching plugin, for example, sends search terms such as title and author to the site it queries.

Kindle features. When you manage a Kindle over USB, data moves only between your computer and the device. When you use Send to Kindle, you sign in to and use Amazon’s service directly inside the app, and Amazon handles that data under its own policies.

Feedback. The in-app feedback entry only links to GitHub Issues and the user group; the app never attaches anything automatically. Content you post in these public channels may be seen by others, so please check logs and screenshots for personal information before sharing.

4. Information handled by the account service

Creating and signing in to a Libzora account is optional. If you use an account, the account service handles:

  • Account details: your username, email address and a securely processed sign-in password;
  • Devices and sessions: a random device identifier generated by the app for each device (no hardware serial numbers are read), device name and type, sign-in time and last access time, used to show and manage your signed-in devices;
  • Entitlement status: your account’s trial and entitlement status and their validity period;
  • Security information: the IP address used when registering and signing in, used to limit bulk registration, prevent abuse and keep accounts secure.

This information is used only for authentication, email verification and password recovery, device management, entitlement checks and account security. The account service does not receive your books, covers, reading data, annotations, synced data, sync key, storage credentials or AI API keys.

Accounts belong to either the China region or the International region. Account data for the two regions is stored separately and is never copied, merged or shared between them.

5. Website

The Libzora website is a static site. It uses no advertising or analytics scripts and sets no tracking cookies. It stores only your language preference locally in your browser and queries GitHub for the latest release information; installers are downloaded directly from GitHub.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising or profiling. We do not provide account information to third parties except:

  • where necessary to provide the Service, such as sending a verification code to your email address through an email delivery provider;
  • where required by law or by a binding request from a competent authority;
  • with your explicit prior consent.

7. Retention

  • Local data is under your control; uninstalling the app or deleting its data folder removes it;
  • Synced data in your own storage or iCloud is yours to manage and delete. Ceasing to use Libzora does not remove it automatically;
  • Account information is kept for as long as the account exists. When you deactivate your account we revoke all sign-in sessions; we continue to keep the account and entitlement records necessary for security, abuse prevention and legal obligations;
  • Source-address records used to limit bulk registration are kept for no more than 30 days.

8. Your rights

In the app’s account center you can view and change your username, change your password, review and remove signed-in devices, or deactivate your account. You can also use the contact details below to ask to access, correct or delete the account information we hold about you, or to withdraw consent you previously gave; we will handle such requests in accordance with applicable law. Data that exists only on your devices or in your own storage can be exported or deleted by you at any time.

9. Security

Communication between the apps and the account service uses HTTPS; sign-in passwords are not stored in plain text; sensitive settings such as API keys are protected by the operating system’s secure storage; and full data backups and synced data support end-to-end encryption. Please keep your sync key safe: if it is lost, encrypted sync data and backups cannot be decrypted, and resetting your account password will not recover it.

10. Children

If you are under 14 (or under the age of digital consent where you live), please use the Service only with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian, and do not create an account on your own. If we learn that we have collected such information without guardian consent, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date; for material changes we will give prominent notice in the app or on the website.

12. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your personal information, open an issue on GitHub Issues or reach us through the QQ user group 1109898894. Please do not post passwords, keys or other sensitive information in public channels.

See also the Terms of Service.
