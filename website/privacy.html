隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

Libzora 是一款本地优先的电子书阅读与书库管理工具。你的书籍文件、封面、阅读进度、书签及批注默认仅存储在你自己的设备上；应用不内置广告或统计分析组件，也不会自动上传日志或崩溃报告。仅在你主动使用同步、AI、插件、版本检查或账号等联网功能时，才会产生下文所述的必要数据传输。

一、适用范围

本政策适用于 Libzora 的 Windows、macOS、Linux、Android 与 iOS / iPadOS 客户端，Libzora 官网（libzora.stacker.beauty）以及 Libzora 账号服务（以下合称“本服务”）。本服务由 Libzora 开发者 kingstacker（以下称“我们”）提供。

你自行配置并使用的第三方服务（例如自备对象存储、WebDAV 服务、iCloud、AI 服务商及插件访问的外部站点）由各自的服务提供方依据其隐私政策处理数据，不在本政策的适用范围内。

二、保存在你设备上的数据

以下数据均由应用在你的设备本地创建并保存，我们既不会收集也无法访问：

  • 你导入的书籍文件、封面图片与书籍元数据；
  • 阅读进度、阅读时长统计、书签、划线、批注以及读后感；
  • 你添加或下载的词典、字体、本地朗读语音模型等资源；
  • 应用偏好设置，以及你填写的同步连接信息、同步密钥与 AI 服务 API Key（敏感凭据通过操作系统提供的安全存储加密保护）；
  • 用于排查故障的本地软件日志。日志在写入前会进行脱敏处理，仅记录必要的错误事件以及应用版本、系统平台信息，绝不包含书籍正文或阅读内容，也不会自动上传；仅在你主动导出并分享时才会离开设备。

在 iOS / iPadOS 平台上，桌面小组件展示的阅读信息仅在本机的应用与小组件之间共享，不会离开你的设备。

三、何时会联网，以及传输什么

检查更新与资源下载。当应用检查新版本，或下载你选择的朗读语音模型、Calibre 安装指引等资源时，会向 GitHub 和 Libzora 官网发起请求。与访问常规网站相同，目标服务器会收到你的 IP 地址及基础请求信息（如应用版本和系统类型）。此类请求不包含你的书库内容或任何阅读数据。

跨设备同步。开启同步功能后，应用会将书籍、封面、阅读进度、书签和批注直接传输至你自行选择的存储：自备的 S3 兼容存储、WebDAV 服务，或你个人 Apple 账户下的 iCloud。上述数据不经过也不保存在我们的服务器上。设置同步密钥后，数据会先在你的设备上完成加密再上传；同步密钥仅保存在你的设备上，我们不持有且无法找回。需要说明的是，即便启用端到端加密，存储服务提供方仍可能看到文件数量、大致体积和同步时间。

AI 功能。当你使用 AI 阅读助手、翻译等功能时，应用仅会将完成该请求所必需的内容（例如你选中的文字、相关书籍片段和你的提问）直接发送至你自行配置的 AI 服务商，我们不会中转或留存这些内容。

插件。插件仅在你安装并授予网络权限后才能访问网络。例如，书籍信息匹配类插件会将书名、作者或 ISBN 等检索词发送至其所查询的站点。

Kindle 相关功能。通过 USB 管理 Kindle 设备时，数据仅在你的电脑与设备之间本地传输。使用“发送到 Kindle”时，你是在应用内直接登录并使用 Amazon 提供的服务，相关数据由 Amazon 依据其隐私政策处理。

问题反馈。应用内的反馈入口只是跳转至 GitHub Issues 和用户交流群的链接，应用不会自动收集或附带任何信息。你在这些公开渠道发布的内容可能被他人看到，分享日志或截图前请检查并遮蔽其中的个人敏感信息。

四、账号服务处理的信息

注册并登录 Libzora 账号完全可选；不登录账号即可使用全部核心本地功能。如果你选择使用账号，账号服务仅会处理以下必要信息：

  • 账号资料：用户名、邮箱地址以及经过安全处理的登录密码；
  • 设备与登录会话：应用为每台设备随机生成的设备标识（不采集硬件序列号）、设备名称与系统类型、登录时间及最近访问时间，用于展示和管理你的已登录设备；
  • 权益状态：账号关联的试用状态、权益开通情况及其有效期；
  • 安全信息：注册与登录请求时的来源 IP 地址，用于限制批量注册、防范滥用并保障账号安全。

上述信息仅用于身份认证、邮箱验证与密码找回、设备管理、权益校验和账号安全。账号服务绝不接收你的书籍文件、封面、阅读数据、批注、同步内容、同步密钥、存储连接凭据或 AI API Key。

如账号服务提供多个独立的服务器区域，各区域的账号数据均分别隔离存储，互不复制、合并或跨区域互通。

五、官网

Libzora 官网为纯静态页面，不使用广告或第三方统计分析脚本，也不设置用于跟踪的 Cookie。页面仅在你的浏览器本地保存语言偏好，并向 GitHub 查询最新版本信息；安装包直接从 GitHub 下载。

六、我们如何共享信息

我们不会出售或出租你的个人信息，也不会将其用于广告投放或用户画像。除以下情形外，我们不会向第三方提供账号信息：

  • 为提供服务所必需，例如通过邮件发送服务向你的注册邮箱投递验证码或安全通知；
  • 依据可适用的法律法规或有权机关的强制性要求；
  • 事先获得你的明确同意。

七、保存期限

  • 本地数据由你自行保管，删除书籍、清理应用数据目录或卸载应用即可从设备清除；
  • 保存在你自备存储或 iCloud 中的同步数据由你自行管理与删除，停用账号或卸载 Libzora 不会自动清除这些数据；
  • 账号信息在账号存续期间保存。停用账号后，我们会立即撤销全部登录会话；为保障安全、防止滥用并履行法定义务，必要的账号与权益记录仍会保留；
  • 用于限制批量注册的来源地址记录最长保留 30 天，到期后自动清除。

八、你的权利

你可以在应用的账户中心查看和修改用户名、修改密码、查看并移除已登录设备，或停用账号。你也可以通过下方的联系方式，请求查阅、更正或删除我们持有的账号信息，或撤回此前的同意；我们将在合理期限内依据可适用法律处理。对于只保存在你设备或自备存储中的数据，你可以随时直接导出或删除。

九、信息安全

客户端与账号服务之间的通信均采用 HTTPS 加密传输；登录密码不以明文保存；API Key、存储凭据与同步密钥等敏感配置均使用操作系统提供的安全存储进行本机保护；完整数据备份与跨设备同步支持端到端加密。请妥善保管你的同步密钥：遗失后，已加密的同步数据和备份将无法解密，重置账号密码也无法找回它。

十、未成年人

如果你属于所在国家或地区法律规定的未成年人（例如未满 14 周岁或未达到当地数字同意最低年龄），请在监护人的同意和指导下使用本服务，切勿自行注册账号。如果我们发现在未获监护人同意的情况下收集了相关信息，会尽快予以删除。

十一、政策更新

我们可能随功能变化更新本政策。更新后的版本将在本页面发布并更新生效日期；涉及重大变更时，我们会在应用内或官网以显著方式提示。

十二、联系我们

如对本政策或你的个人信息保护有任何疑问或请求，欢迎在 GitHub Issues 留言，或加入 QQ 用户群 1109898894 与我们联系。请勿在公开渠道发布密码、API Key 或同步密钥等敏感信息。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Libzora is a local-first ebook reader and library manager. Your book files, covers, reading progress, bookmarks and annotations stay on your own devices by default. The app contains no advertising or analytics components and never uploads logs or crash reports automatically. Data leaves your device only when you choose to use a networked feature such as sync, AI, plugins, update checks or an account, as described below.

1. Scope

This policy covers the Libzora apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS / iPadOS, the Libzora website (libzora.stacker.beauty) and the Libzora account service (together, the “Service”), provided by kingstacker, the developer of Libzora (“we”, “us”).

Third-party services that you configure and use yourself — such as your own object storage, WebDAV servers, iCloud, AI providers and websites accessed by plugins — handle data under their own privacy policies and are outside the scope of this policy.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is created and kept locally by the app. We neither collect nor have access to it:

  • The book files you import, their covers and metadata;
  • Reading progress, reading-time statistics, bookmarks, highlights, notes and reflections;
  • Dictionaries, fonts, text-to-speech voice models and other resources you add or download;
  • App preferences, plus the sync connection details, sync key and AI API keys you enter (sensitive credentials are protected by the secure storage your operating system provides);
  • A local software log used for troubleshooting. Entries are redacted before writing and record only essential error events along with app version and platform information — never book content. The log is never uploaded automatically and leaves your device only if you export and share it yourself.

On iOS / iPadOS, the reading information shown in Home Screen widgets is shared only between the app and its widgets on the same device and never leaves your device.

3. When the app goes online and what it sends

Update checks and resource downloads. When the app checks for a new version, or downloads resources you choose such as voice models or Calibre setup information, it contacts GitHub and the Libzora website. As with any web request, those servers receive your IP address and basic request details such as the app version and operating system. These requests contain nothing from your library or reading data.

Sync across devices. When you turn on sync, the app transfers books, covers, reading progress, bookmarks and annotations directly to storage you choose: your own S3-compatible storage, WebDAV, or iCloud under your Apple Account. The data does not pass through and is not stored on our servers. If you set a sync key, data is encrypted on your device before upload; the key stays on your devices, and we neither hold it nor can recover it. Even with end-to-end encryption, your storage provider may still see the number of files, their approximate sizes and when syncs happen.

AI features. When you use the AI reading assistant, translation or similar features, the app sends only the content needed for that request — such as the text you selected, relevant passages from the book and your question — directly to the AI provider you configured. We do not relay or store this content.

Plugins. Plugins can access the network only after you install them and grant permission. A metadata-matching plugin, for example, sends search terms such as title, author or ISBN to the site it queries.

Kindle features. When you manage a Kindle over USB, data moves only locally between your computer and the device. When you use Send to Kindle, you sign in to and use Amazon’s service directly inside the app, and Amazon handles that data under its own privacy policies.

Feedback. The in-app feedback entry only links to GitHub Issues and the user group; the app never collects or attaches anything automatically. Content you post in these public channels may be seen by others, so please review logs and screenshots and redact any personal information before sharing.

4. Information handled by the account service

Creating and signing in to a Libzora account is entirely optional; all core local features work without an account. If you choose to use an account, the account service handles only the information necessary:

  • Account details: your username, email address and a securely processed sign-in password;
  • Devices and sessions: a random device identifier generated by the app for each device (no hardware serial numbers are collected), device name and platform type, sign-in time and last access time, used to display and manage your signed-in devices;
  • Entitlement status: your account’s trial and entitlement status and validity period;
  • Security information: the source IP address used when registering and signing in, used solely to limit bulk registration, prevent abuse and keep accounts secure.

This information is used exclusively for authentication, email verification and password recovery, device management, entitlement checks and account security. The account service never receives your books, covers, reading data, annotations, synced content, sync key, storage credentials or AI API keys.

Where separate server regions are available, account data for each region is stored separately in isolation and is never copied, merged or shared across regions.

5. Website

The Libzora website is a static site. It uses no advertising or third-party analytics scripts and sets no tracking cookies. It stores only your language preference locally in your browser and queries GitHub for the latest release information; installers are downloaded directly from GitHub.

6. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information or use it for advertising or profiling. We do not provide account information to third parties except:

  • where necessary to provide the Service, such as delivering a verification code or security notice to your email address through an email delivery provider;
  • where required by applicable law or by a binding request from a competent authority;
  • with your explicit prior consent.

7. Retention

  • Local data is under your control; deleting books, clearing the app data folder or uninstalling the app removes it from your device;
  • Synced data in your own storage or iCloud is yours to manage and delete; deactivating your account or uninstalling Libzora does not remove it automatically;
  • Account information is kept for as long as the account exists. When you deactivate your account we immediately revoke all sign-in sessions; we retain the account and entitlement records necessary for security, abuse prevention and legal obligations;
  • Source-address records used to limit bulk registration are kept for no more than 30 days and are deleted thereafter.

8. Your rights

In the app’s account center you can view and change your username, change your password, review and remove signed-in devices, or deactivate your account. You can also use the contact details below to request access to, correction of or deletion of the account information we hold about you, or to withdraw prior consent; we will handle such requests within a reasonable time in accordance with applicable law. Data that exists only on your devices or in your own storage can be exported or deleted by you at any time.

9. Security

Communication between the apps and the account service uses HTTPS; sign-in passwords are never stored in plain text; sensitive settings such as API keys, storage credentials and sync keys are protected locally by the operating system’s secure storage; and full data backups and synced data support end-to-end encryption. Please keep your sync key safe: if it is lost, encrypted sync data and backups cannot be decrypted, and resetting your account password will not recover it.

10. Children

If you are a minor under the laws of your country or region (such as under 14 or under the applicable age of digital consent), please use the Service only with the consent and guidance of a parent or legal guardian, and do not create an account on your own. If we learn that we have collected such information without guardian consent, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features evolve. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date; for material changes we will give prominent notice in the app or on the website.

12. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your personal information, open an issue on GitHub Issues or reach us through the QQ user group 1109898894. Never post passwords, API keys, sync keys or other sensitive information in public channels.

See also the Terms of Service.
